Privacy Policy, Matchstick Puzzle

Effective date: November 7, 2026

In short

  • Matchstick Puzzle has no account and no server of our own. We do not receive your game data.
  • If you use Google Play Games, Google keeps your leaderboard scores and achievements under your Play Games profile. It is optional and the game works the same without it.
  • Your progress, daily streak and settings are stored on your device. The daily reminder is optional, off until you turn it on, and runs entirely on your device.
  • Ads are shown using Google's ad SDK (Google AdMob). Google collects certain data, such as your IP address and advertising ID, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This is described below.
  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked before personalized ads are used, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings, Privacy choices.
  • An optional one time purchase removes ads between levels and makes hints free. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Who we are

Matchstick Puzzle (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is published by manextapps. This policy explains how the Android app handles information. For questions, see Contact.

2. About the app

Matchstick Puzzle is a logic puzzle game: you move, add or remove matches to make an equation true, or to leave an exact number of squares or triangles. It includes four level packs, a daily puzzle with a day streak, optional hints, themes and settings. The levels ship inside the app and every answer is checked on your device. An internet connection is used only for ads, for the ad consent form, for the optional purchase, for Google Play Games if you use it, and when you choose to open or share a link.

3. Data we collect

We, the developer, do not operate a server for this app and do not collect, receive or store personal data about you. There's no account of our own, no analytics or crash reporting tool of our own, and no Firebase.

Ads in the app are provided by Google, which collects some data directly through its software development kit (SDK). That is explained in section 5. The optional purchase is processed by Google Play, see section 8. If you use Google Play Games, Google stores your scores and achievements, see section 9.

If you email us, we will receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

4. Data stored on your device

To make the game work, the following information is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to us.

  • Game progress: which levels you solved in each pack, the last level you opened, and the level you are in the middle of.
  • Daily puzzle results and your daily streak, the time you spent on today's daily puzzle and how many hints you used.
  • The answers you found on puzzles that have more than one answer.
  • Your daily reminder choice and time, and the day it last reminded you.
  • A short record of what was already sent to Google Play Games, so the same result isn't sent twice.
  • Counters used to pace ads (for example, how many levels you solved since the last full screen ad).
  • Settings: theme, sound and vibration, and whether ads were removed by a purchase.

Google's ad, consent and billing libraries also store some information on your device, such as your consent choices, so that they work correctly.

5. Advertising and Google's ad SDK

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request ad consent where required. The app shows:

  • Rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one to get a hint. If no ad is available, you still get the hint. You never have to watch an ad to keep playing.
  • Full screen (interstitial) ads, only when you leave a solved level, at most once every three solved levels and never during a level or in your first session. These stop after the remove ads purchase.

Non personalized ads are based on more limited context, such as the app itself and your general (not precise) location. 6. Consent and your ad choices

Where it is required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and, where enabled, certain U.S. states), the app shows Google's consent message when it starts. You can accept, decline or manage your choices.

When privacy options are available for your region, you can review or change your choices at any time in the app under Settings, Privacy choices. If your choices mean ads cannot be requested, the game still works and hints are still available.

You can also manage ad personalization for your Google Account at My Ad Center.

7. Your advertising ID

Android provides a resettable advertising ID. On most devices you can reset or delete it in Settings, Privacy, Ads (menu names vary by device and Android version). Deleting it stops apps from accessing it for advertising; resetting it replaces it with a new random ID. Some devices also offer ad privacy settings for the Android Privacy Sandbox in the same area.

8. In app purchase

You can buy a one time “Remove ads” upgrade. The purchase is made through Google Play Billing: Google handles the payment and your payment details, and we never see them. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the upgrade belongs to your Google account, so it can turn off full screen ads, give hints without a video and restore the purchase on a new device. Google's handling of purchases is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms.

9. Google Play Games

The app supports Google Play Games Services for leaderboards and achievements. It's optional. If you have a Play Games profile on your phone, Google signs you in automatically when the game starts; if you don't, or you never sign in, the game works exactly the same and your progress stays on your device.

When you are signed in, the app sends these results to Google Play Games:

  • Your time on the daily puzzle (the first solve of each day, with 30 seconds added for each hint).
  • Your current daily streak and the number of levels you solved.
  • Achievement progress, such as levels solved per mode, streak length and answers found.

Google links this to your Play Games profile (your gamer name and avatar) and shows it to other players according to your Play Games profile visibility setting: only you, your friends, or everyone. The app reads back your rank on the daily leaderboard to show it to you. We, the developer, can see leaderboard entries the way players see them, and we receive no other data about you from Google Play Games.

According to Google, Play Games data is encrypted in transit, and you can delete it in your Play Games profile or your Google Account. More about it is in the Google Privacy Policy and in Google's data disclosure for Play Games Services.

10. Daily reminder notification

You can turn on one reminder a day for the daily puzzle. It's off until you turn it on, and on Android 13 and later Android asks for your permission first. The reminder is scheduled on your device, comes at most once a day, and doesn't come at all if you already solved the puzzle. Nothing about it is sent to us or to anyone else. You can change the time or turn it off in the app's Settings or in Android's notification settings.

11. Android permissions

You won't be asked for runtime permissions such as location, camera, contacts or storage. The only permission the app may ask for is notifications, and only when you turn on the daily reminder. The installed app includes the following permissions:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the optional daily reminder. Android asks you first, on Android 13 and later.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): keeps the daily reminder scheduled after your phone restarts. Nothing runs at startup unless the reminder is on.
  • Install referrer (com.google.android.finsky.permission.BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE): lets the app read, once after install, which shared puzzle link brought you to Google Play, so it can open that puzzle. It stays on your device.
  • Internet access and network state: to load ads, the consent form, the purchase screen and Google Play Games.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): lets the ad SDK read the advertising ID.
  • Android ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): let the ad SDK use Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features where the device supports them.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): lets the app offer the optional purchase.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: required by the Android WorkManager library that the ad SDK uses for background tasks. Matchstick Puzzle itself doesn't run a foreground service.

12. Children

The app is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app itself does not collect personal information from anyone. Ads in the app are served by Google, which collects the data described in section 5. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions or concerns, please contact us.

13. Data retention and deletion

  • Our servers: we have none, so we keep no copy of your game data.
  • On your device: game data stays until you clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app.
  • Google Play Games: your scores and achievements stay in your Play Games profile until you delete them there or delete your Play Games profile.
  • Google: data collected by Google's ad SDK and by Google Play is retained according to Google's policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google retains data.

14. Device backup

Android's backup feature may include your game progress and settings, so they can be restored if you set up a new device. If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may store this data in your Google Account backup or move it during a device to device transfer, under Google's terms and your device settings. Data stored by the ad and consent libraries is excluded from backup. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

15. Sharing and links

Your personal data is never sold or shared by us, because we never receive any. Apart from Google's libraries described above, the app sends information only when you choose to:

  • Open a link, such as this privacy policy or another of our games on Google Play. This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This page uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Contact us: the app opens your email app with our address filled in. You decide what to send.
  • Share a puzzle: the share button opens Android's share sheet with the puzzle as text and a link to a page on manextapps.com. You choose the app and the person; we don't see who you share with. The link names the puzzle and nothing else.
  • Open a shared link: if a friend sends you a puzzle link and you install the game from it, Google Play passes a referrer code that names that puzzle and the source "friend_share". The app reads it once on your device to open the puzzle. Google Play also counts installs per source for us as totals, without telling us who you are.

16. Security

Because we do not collect your data, there is no copy of it on our side to protect or lose. Game data on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox. Google states that the data its ad SDK collects is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

17. Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741 (1981), and U.S. state privacy laws may give you rights over your personal data. These can include the right to access, correct or delete it, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority.

  • We do not hold personal data about you, so in most cases there is nothing for us to access or delete. Your game data is on your device and you can delete it as described in section 13.
  • For data collected by Google, use your ad choices in the app (section 6), your device's ad settings (section 7), and Google's tools described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can always contact us, and we will help as far as we are able.
